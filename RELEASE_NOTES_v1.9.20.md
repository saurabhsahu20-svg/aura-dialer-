S Caller v1.9.20 — Direct chat controls

This update makes WhatsApp and Telegram directly accessible from Contacts, Favorites, Call history, Contact detail, and Keypad. Previously they were hidden inside the Message dialog. Buttons send explicit user-selected intents to installed apps first, then use public web links as fallback. Calling/SMS and hangup fixes stay.

The other messaging app verifies account eligibility and privacy restrictions. Device tests remain necessary. Debug signing may differ from earlier APKs.
