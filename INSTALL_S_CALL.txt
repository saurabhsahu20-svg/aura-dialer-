IMPORTANT: HOW TO FIX A RINGING CALL WITH NO VISIBLE CALL SCREEN

1. Open S call > Settings > Incoming call display.
2. Tap Fix if any requirement is missing.
3. Make S call the DEFAULT PHONE APP in Android Settings.
4. Open Settings > Apps > S call > Notifications and allow its phone-call notifications.
5. On Android 14+ (if asked), allow Full-screen notifications in Special app access > Full-screen intents for S call.
6. Confirm the Phone calls notification channel is allowed and can pop on screen.
7. Test an incoming call while your phone is unlocked (heads-up alert) and again when locked (full call screen when allowed).

Android deliberately decides whether to show a heads-up call alert or the full-screen activity;
a third-party dialer cannot override system or user-disabled notification permissions.
This preview APK has NOT been validated with a real carrier incoming call. Keep the stock
Phone app available, especially for emergency calling.

CROSS-VERSION INSTALL SUPPORT
- Android 6.0+ (Marshmallow and newer) is the minimum supported OS.
- Android 5.x and earlier: unsupported because the InCallService API is unavailable.
- Android 6 and 7: no notification channels; incoming call notifications use Android-compatible legacy alerts.
- Android 8+: use the Phone calls notification channel.
- Android 13+: allow Notifications; Android 14+: check Full-screen call alerts.
- Cellular calls require compatible telephony hardware and default-phone-role permission.
- Android phones and manufacturers may differ even on the same OS version.

S CALL v1.8.2 — AUDIO ROUTING & VOICE QUALITY

During a connected call, tap "Audio" to select only the wired headset,
Bluetooth device, phone earpiece, or speaker currently reported by Android.
Use "Speaker" for a quick toggle; the app prefers available wired audio
when switching speakerphone off. Actual selection can still be rejected
by Android Telecom, and the call screen will show a clear error.

Settings > Voice quality & audio capabilities is an informational check.
Noise suppression, echo cancellation and automatic gain control
reported as available are for APP-OWNED VoIP audio sessions only;
THEY ARE NOT ACTIVE ON REGULAR SIM/CELLULAR CALLS.
Real SIM audio quality depends on phone hardware, network and carrier.

Keep the stock phone app available for emergency calls.

CALL RECORDING LIBRARY — v1.8.2
Open S call > Settings > Call Recording.
- Standard SIM automatic incoming/outgoing recording is NOT supported by S call.
- If your built-in dialer has recorded a call with proper notice/consent,
  choose Import and select that audio file using the Android picker.
- Manage private copies: Search, Play/Stop, Rename, Share and Delete.
- Your original file in the other app is not deleted when you delete the imported copy.
- Optional cleanup (30/90 days) runs only when you choose Clean up and confirm.
- No hidden microphone, live two-way call capture, VoIP recorder or AI recording exists.
- Export important files before uninstalling (app-private copies are removed).
