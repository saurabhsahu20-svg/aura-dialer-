# S call v1.1.0 — Caller Photo Studio Preview

**S call** is an Android phone dialer with a minimal, premium Material 3 interface.

## ✨ What's new
- **Redesigned Caller Photo Studio** with a large live incoming-call preview
- Select a contact picture from Android's document picker and **preview before saving**
- **Pinch to zoom** and **drag to reposition** the caller photo
- **Zoom slider**, **Tone gradient slider**, **Reset**, **Save look**, and **Remove photo**
- Saved image framing applied to real incoming/outgoing call screens
- Quick **photo shortcut** directly in Contacts and Favorites
- Refined offline Android system **sans-serif typography** for readable names and numbers
- All existing calling controls, swipe gestures, phone contacts, themes and blue S call icon remain

## First use
1. Download and install the attached APK on an Android phone.
2. Grant appropriate app permissions and choose S call as the default phone app.
3. In **Contacts**, tap the **photo icon**, select an image, pinch/drag to frame it and tap **Save look**.
4. Test incoming and outgoing calls using another device before using the app as your everyday dialer.

## Known limitations
- Real-device calling, lock-screen, multi-SIM and notification behavior have **not** been fully tested.
- The full-screen caller interface is subject to Android permissions and device policies.
- Phone features such as merging calls and audio routing depend on carrier/device capabilities.
- Online unknown-number caller lookup and recording are **not included**.
- For emergency calls, do not rely on an untested alternative dialer; keep the preinstalled phone app available.
